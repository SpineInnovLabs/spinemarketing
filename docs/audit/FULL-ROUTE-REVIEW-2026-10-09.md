This report supersedes the incomplete source-only review of 8 October for website rendering and route coverage.

## Why the site became renewal-only

A shared `BUYER_HERO_BY_PATH` override replaced page-specific introductions, and a renewal-first fallback injected the same audience and first workflow into unrelated functions. `PageHero` applied that layer automatically. Route/link checks preserved URLs but did not catch the content scope or white-on-white legacy heroes. The override and injected buyer band have been removed. Page introductions and calls to action now come from each page's own copy.

Twin describes investigation, preparation and coordination across operations, finance, product/engineering, customer-facing teams, leadership, and people/knowledge. Renewal is one customer-facing example. Fabric describes relationships, provenance, authority and continuity across business work. The company site and the local `/product` feature page remain distinct.
