The Data Pipeline

Loader → Normalizer → Spine + Context → Entity 360 → Twin
How raw, fragmented tool data becomes unified canonical truth — available to every AI session and every user in real time.

1
Loader
Pulls raw data from every connected tool via APIs, webhooks, streaming events, and file drops. Idempotent ingestion with dead-letter queuing.

2
Normalizer
Validates, parses, deduplicates, and maps vendor-specific payloads to canonical Spine schema. Golden record creation with conflict resolution.

3
Spine + Context
Normalized entities (Account, Contact, Deal, Ticket) persist in the SSOT with full versioning. Relationship graphs and temporal markers enrich every record.

4
Entity 360
A queryable, living object assembled in real-time from Spine — CRM health, billing, tickets, usage, and stakeholder signals unified in one view.

5
Twin
The Cognitive Layer reads from Spine continuously, detecting cross-system signals and building reasoning chains grounded in canonical entity data.

The Intelligence Layer

AI Session Summaries → Triage Bot → Knowledge UI → Entity 360 → Twin
How your team's interactions become structured knowledge — and how the Twin compounds that knowledge into sharper intelligence with every approved session.

1
AI Session Summaries
Every customer call, email thread, and meeting auto-summarized, linked to entity records, and persisted in Spine as structured knowledge — not lost in inbox history.

2
Triage Bot
Monitors the Spine for risk signals, renewal flags, and anomalies across every account. Surfaces prioritized actions with confidence scores and evidence chains.

3
Knowledge UI
The Workspace surface where approved insights, session memory, and entity context live side-by-side. No context-switching — everything your team needs is one view.

4
Entity 360
AI and human context converge at the Entity 360 — the live account, contact, or deal view that combines system data with session intelligence.

5
Twin
Your AI counterpart that learns from every approved session, compounds cross-account insights, and gets sharper with every approval and rejection.

The Twin learns from every approved session.

Every approval, rejection, and edit feeds back into the reasoning layer. The more your team uses it, the sharper it gets — across all accounts, not just the one in view.
