## IntegrateWise – What It Looks Like on the Front‑Line

**In plain language:**  
IntegrateWise is a single, shared workspace where every tool you already use (CRM, support desk, billing, product analytics, email, docs, Slack, etc.) talks to each other automatically. The system builds a **live “360‑degree view” of every customer, deal, project or product**, surfaces AI‑driven warnings **before** something goes wrong, suggests the exact next step **with the proof it’s based on**, and then **waits for a human to approve** before any system change is made.  

All of this happens without you having to write code, build data pipelines, or copy‑paste information from one app to another.

## Core Features – The Language Your Team Uses Every Day  

| Feature (User‑friendly name) | What the user sees / does | Why it matters |
|------------------------------|---------------------------|----------------|
| **Unified Customer/Account View** | A single page that pulls together data from Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk, Stripe, Google Drive, Slack, product telemetry, etc. – no tabs, no log‑ins. | Stops hunting across 8+ tools; every piece of context is right there when you need it. |
| **Live “Entity 360” Dashboard** | Real‑time health score, latest usage, open tickets, recent emails, contract terms, and even AI conversation snippets for any account or project. | Gives you a complete, up‑to‑date picture instantly – you always know the truth. |
| **Proactive Signal Engine** | AI‑driven alerts (e.g., “Usage dropped 30 % last week and the champion hasn’t replied”) appear in a **Signal Center** with a one‑click “Investigate” button. | Catches risk or opportunity weeks before a renewal or escalation, letting you act early. |
| **Evidence‑Backed Recommendations** | When a signal fires, the AI shows **exact source records** (email thread, ticket, usage chart) and a short reasoning paragraph. You can accept, edit, or reject. | Trust the AI – you see the data that led to the suggestion, not a black‑box guess. |
| **Approval‑First Action Card** | Every recommended action (send an email, update a CRM field, create a task, change a contract status) is wrapped in a **Review & Approve** card that only a manager, finance lead, or exec can click “Approve”. | Guarantees compliance, auditability, and human control – no rogue writes. |
| **One‑Click Execution** | After approval, the platform automatically writes back to the right system(s): CRM, billing, ticketing, Slack, etc. No manual data entry. | Saves time, eliminates human error, and closes the loop instantly. |
| **Learning Loop** | The system records whether you approved or rejected each suggestion and uses that feedback to fine‑tune future signals for your specific team. | The AI gets better the more you use it, and it learns your unique business rules. |
| **Workspace + Permissions** | Teams (CS, Sales, Finance, Product, Ops) each get a tailored view and role‑based access; the same Spine powers all workspaces. | Keeps data secure and relevant to each department while sharing the same source of truth. |
| **Connector Marketplace** | 30+ out‑of‑the‑box connectors (Salesforce, HubSpot, Intercom, Zendesk, Stripe, Snowflake, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Atlassian, etc.) plus private API hooks. | Hook up any tool you already own in minutes; no custom integration project. |
