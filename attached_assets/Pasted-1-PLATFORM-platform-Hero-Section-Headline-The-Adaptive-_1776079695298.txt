Section: The Five Dimensions
The architectural differentiators that create the whitespace IntegrateWise occupies.
1. Spine Equivalent (Vendor-Neutral SSOT)
Unlike iPaaS tools that only move data or CRMs that lock truth in proprietary schemas, the Spine maintains a normalized, vendor-independent canonical layer.
Canonical Entities: Account, Contact, Deal, Ticket, Invoice, Project exist as neutral objects, not Salesforce rows or HubSpot records.
Live Alignment: Continuous bidirectional sync with conflict resolution, not batch ETL.
Schema Evolution: Automatic drift detection when upstream APIs change; field mapping versioned and governed.
Result: Your data has a home independent of any vendor's roadmap or pricing change.
2. Entity 360 (Live Cross-System View)
Not a dashboard. Not a report. A queryable, living object that assembles in real-time.
Account 360: CRM status + billing health + open tickets + product usage + recent stakeholder emails + renewal timeline.
Conflict Resolution: When two systems disagree (e.g., Salesforce vs. QuickBooks revenue), the Spine flags it; AI suggests resolution based on source-of-record rules you define.
Temporal Truth: Point-in-time reconstruction—see exactly what was known on any historical date.
3. Proactive Signal Detection
AI monitors the Spine continuously, not on-demand.
Cross-System Patterns: "Usage dropped 30% AND champion left AND ticket volume spiked" = Churn risk before the CRM shows it.
Confidence Scoring: Every signal carries a confidence score (0-100) based on data freshness and source reliability.
Noise Suppression: Low-confidence or stale signals are quarantined, not surfaced.
4. Reasoning with Evidence (The Think Layer)
A dedicated cognitive layer that shows its work.
Chain-of-Thought: AI explains why it flagged an account as "At Risk," with hyperlinks to source records across systems.
Dual Context Mapping: Every recommendation explicitly states impact on both vendor goals (e.g., Salesforce data quality) and client business goals (e.g., renewal probability).
Evidence Anchoring: No insight without goal_refs linking back to canonical Spine entities.
5. Non-Bypassable HITL (Human-in-the-Loop)
Human approval is an architectural gate, not a workflow add-on.
Approval Topology: All AI-generated actions route through the TruthLayer—a governance membrane that requires human authorization before any write operation.
Audit Immutability: Every approval/rejection/edit is append-only, signed, and timestamped in the Spine's decision log.
Emergency Overrides: Even admin "force" actions create traceable exceptions, never silent executions.
Section: The 8-Stage Pipeline
How data flows from chaos to governed intelligence.
Table
Stage	Function	Human Touchpoint
1. Ingest	Webhooks, APIs, email ingestion, file drops	Connector configuration approval
2. Normalize	Schema mapping, deduplication, canonicalization	Schema drift review
3. Store	Spine SSOT, versioned entities, relation graphs	Data retention governance
4. Render	Workspace views, Entity 360 assembly	View customization
5. Think	AI signal detection, pattern analysis, reasoning	Confidence threshold tuning
6. Govern (HITL)	TruthLayer scoring, approval routing, evidence display	Mandatory approval gate
7. Act	Write-backs to connected systems, task creation	Execute or reschedule
8. Re-ingest	Action outcomes flow back to Spine as new truth	Outcome validation
