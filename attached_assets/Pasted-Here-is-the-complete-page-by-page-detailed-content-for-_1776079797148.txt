# **SOLUTIONS ARCHITECTURE**

## **By Function**

### **1. Account Success** `/solutions/account-success`
*For CSMs, Account Managers, and Growth teams managing B2B relationships*

**Hero**  
**Headline:** Save accounts. Accelerate renewals. Never miss a signal.  
**Subheadline:** One Account 360 that unifies CRM, product usage, billing, and support—where AI surfaces churn risk and expansion opportunity with evidence, and every save play waits for your approval.  
**CTA:** See Account Success workspace →

**The Problem**  
You manage 50-100 accounts. To prepare for a QBR, you open Salesforce, Zendesk, Stripe, your product analytics tool, Gmail, and Slack. By the time you’ve assembled context, the meeting started—or the renewal already slipped.  
- Risk hides in the gaps between usage drop and support tickets.  
- Expansion opportunity is invisible when billing lives in QuickBooks and activity lives in Salesforce.  
- You write the same "checking in" emails because you don't have the full health picture.

**The Solution**  
IntegrateWise gives CSMs a **Account Command Center**—a single workspace where the Spine assembles the complete customer story from every connected system. The Twin reads this unified picture, detects patterns that indicate risk or growth potential, and prepares recommended actions. But nothing sends until you approve it.

**Core Capabilities**  
- **Renewal Command Center:** 90/60/30-day pipeline with health indicators drawn from cross-system signals (usage + billing + sentiment).  
- **Champion Lifecycle Tracking:** Automatic stakeholder mapping with departure alerts; when your champion leaves, AI flags the account for immediate attention.  
- **QBR Auto-Prep:** One-click generation of business review decks using real product adoption data, outcome metrics, and relationship history—not CRM guesses.  
- **Save Play Workspace:** AI suggests specific interventions based on risk patterns (e.g., "Usage down + Sponsor gone + Payment late = High churn risk"), drafts the outreach, and queues it for your edit and approval.

**Governance**  
Every health score change, every escalation suggestion, every renewal forecast displays its source data and confidence score. You approve or edit before any customer communication sends. Full audit trail: "CSM approved risk escalation on [Date] based on Spine confidence score 87/100."

**Connects:** Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk, Intercom, Stripe, Chargebee, product analytics (Segment, Amplitude), Slack, Gmail.  
**KPIs:** Net Revenue Retention (NRR), Gross Revenue Retention (GRR), Time-to-Intervention, Expansion Rate, Renewal Forecast Accuracy.

### **2. Business Ops** `/solutions/business-ops`
*For COOs, RevOps, and Operations leaders who need the live operating picture*

**Hero**  
**Headline:** The live operating picture. Governed.  
**Subheadline:** Revenue, risk, and operations unified in one executive workspace. AI explains what changed across your systems this week and proposes actions—waiting for your approval before any system updates.  
**CTA:** Explore Business Ops →

**The Problem**  
Your business truth is fragmented across 12+ tools. Your Monday morning involves logging into Salesforce, Stripe, Zendesk, Asana, and three different spreadsheets to understand if you're on track. By Wednesday, the picture is outdated.  
- Revenue leaks appear first in billing, last in CRM.  
- Escalations start in support but don't reach leadership until they're fires.  
- Board decks require 6 hours of manual data stitching—and you still don't trust the numbers.

**The Solution**  
Business Ops is a **command center** connecting pipeline, bookings, billings, collections, delivery status, and customer health into one governed view. The Spine normalizes data from CRM, finance, support, and project systems. The Twin detects operational patterns (process blocks, margin leaks, cash flow risks) and prepares executive briefs and suggested interventions. TruthLayer ensures every proposed change—whether a process fix or a data correction—waits for human approval.

**Core Capabilities**  
- **Weekly Operating Brief:** AI-generated one-page narrative of business changes (revenue movement, at-risk accounts, collection priorities, delivery exceptions) with evidence from source systems.  
- **Revenue & Cash Cockpit:** Real-time view of ARR, MRR, collections, and churn risk with drill-down to account-level context.  
- **Exception-Based Management:** Surface only what requires human judgment—AI suppresses noise, highlights true anomalies (e.g., "Deal stalled in 'Negotiation' for 45 days with no stakeholder activity").  
- **Cross-Functional Alignment:** Shared definitions across Sales, CS, and Finance. One definition of "At-Risk," sourced from the same Spine data.

**Governance**  
Process changes, health score adjustments, and automated workflows all route through approval gates. RevOps maintains control over definitions; executives maintain control over actions. Immutable logs show who approved what operational change and when.

**Connects:** Salesforce, HubSpot, QuickBooks, Stripe, Zendesk, Jira, Asana, Monday.com, NetSuite, Slack.  
**KPIs:** Cash Conversion Cycle, Pipeline Coverage, Forecast Accuracy, Collection Efficiency, Gross Margin per Project.

**Subheadline:** Tasks, follow-ups, emails, and threads from all your apps unified in one calm view. AI prepares your morning brief and suggests next actions—never executing without your explicit approval.

**The Problem**

- Follow-ups fall through cracks because they live in different tools.  
- You miss critical context in meetings because you can't assemble the full story fast enough.  
- AI assistants act without your knowledge, sending messages or creating tasks you didn't authorize.

**The Solution**  
Personal Ops is your **individual command center**—a L1 workspace aggregating your specific tasks, commitments, and priorities from across the organization's tools. The Twin reads your Spine-connected data and provides a morning brief, session memory, and cross-tool search. But unlike "agent" tools, it cannot send emails, update tickets, or move deals without your explicit click.

**Core Capabilities**  
- **Morning Brief:** 2-minute AI-generated digest of what changed overnight in your accounts, projects, and priorities—pulled from email, Slack, CRM, and task tools.  
- **Follow-Up Memory:** "You promised to check on this 4 days ago across three different threads. Here's the current status and suggested next step."  
- **Meeting Prep:** Instant pre-meeting briefs with recent emails, ticket updates, deal changes, and stakeholder notes for the specific attendees.  
- **Focus Mode:** AI-suggested priority queue based on deadlines, stakeholder seniority, and business impact—not just due dates.

**Governance**  
Personal but governed. AI can draft your responses, but cannot send them. It can suggest tasks, but cannot create them in your name until you approve. Your personal workspace respects organizational data permissions—you only see what the Spine's RBAC allows.

**Connects:** Gmail, Outlook, Slack, Salesforce, HubSpot, Notion, Asana, Jira, Google Calendar, Zoom.  
**KPIs:** Response Time, Task Completion Velocity, Meeting Preparation Quality, Context-Switching Reduction.
