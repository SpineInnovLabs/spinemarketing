## Platform — Landing Copy

**Headline**
Built on truth, context, and governed memory.

**Sub-headline**
The IntegrateWise Platform ingests records from your systems, context from your conversations, and approved session summaries—then turns them into a reliable intelligence layer that scores confidence and never writes back without human approval.

**Core sections**

1. **Connectors & Ingestion** – pull structured data from CRM, billing, support, docs, chat, and more.
2. **Context Engine** – attach conversations, notes, and decisions where they belong.
3. **Session Summaries** – reuse only what humans approve.
4. **TruthLayer** – score freshness, cross-reference, context coverage, and overall confidence.
5. **Governance & Approvals** – every action and memory update waits for explicit sign-off.
6. **Security** – SSO, RBAC, audit trail, encryption-at-rest.

## Product — Landing Copy

**Headline**
A workbench for daily work. Intelligence underneath.

**Sub-headline**
Users work in the **Workbench**—tasks, accounts, projects, and execution. **Intelligence** reads truth, context, and approved summaries, scores confidence with TruthLayer, and waits for approval before anything happens.

**Key components**

1. **Workbench** – dashboards, timelines, workflows, tasks.
2. **Intelligence** – reasoning, suggestions, summaries.
3. **Approvals Interface** – evidence, confidence, approve / edit / reject.
4. **Unified Timeline** – system events + human context side by side.
5. **Reusable Memory** – approved summaries compound over time.

## Solutions — Landing Copy

**Headline**
Workspaces built around real operating use cases.

**Sub-headline**
Whether you manage customers, run operations, or organize personal execution, IntegrateWise combines truth, context, and approved summaries into a workbench that helps you move faster without losing control.

### Account Success

* **Workbench** shows health, usage, billing, support, and stakeholder signals in one place.
* **Intelligence** surfaces churn risk, expansion opportunity, and drafts follow-ups—with evidence and confidence scores.
* **Outcome**: less context-hunting, earlier risk detection, faster renewals.

### Business Ops

* **Workbench** tracks workflows, hand-offs, metrics, and exceptions across teams.
* **Intelligence** spots gaps, conflicting records, and stale processes; proposes fixes; waits for approval.
* **Outcome**: cleaner data, smoother operations, fewer fire-drills.

### Personal Ops

* **Workbench** unifies tasks, notes, calendar, goals.
* **Intelligence** remembers decisions, summarizes sessions, suggests next actions with continuity.
* **Outcome**: one place for personal execution, powered by context-aware AI.
