## How intelligence helps (L2 Layer)

- AI reads the Spine and:  
  - Summarizes yesterday’s activity, highlights what needs attention, and suggests a simple plan for today.  
  - Groups related threads, docs, and tasks under the same account or project so you stop hunting for context.  
  - Drafts responses, updates, and notes for you to edit and approve, instead of starting from a blank page.  
  - Learns which kinds of work you prioritize, so future suggestions match how you actually operate. [ppl-ai-file-upload.s3.amazonaws](https://ppl-ai-file-upload.s3.amazonaws.com/web/direct-files/attachments/130797605/8c393b7e-2933-432e-bdd6-4a3023e9867c/paste.txt)
