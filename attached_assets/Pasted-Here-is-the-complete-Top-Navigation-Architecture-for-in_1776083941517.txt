### **1. PLATFORM** `/platform`
*The Technical Architecture—The Engine Room*

**Dropdown Structure:**
- **Architecture** `/platform/architecture` — The Four Pillars (Truth, Context, AI Sessions, Entity 360)
- **The Spine** `/platform/the-spine` — SpineVault, 8-Stage Normalization, Canonical Entities

**For:** CTOs, Engineering, Security Teams, Technical Evaluators

## **CTA PLACEMENT**

**Primary CTA (Persistent Right Side):**  
`[Book a demo]` → Links to `/demo-request` or `#demo-form`

**Secondary CTA (Near Solutions):**  
`[See your use case]` → Links to `/solutions/by-role` selector

**Footer Navigation:**  
Mirrors top nav with additional: Documentation, API Reference, Status Page, Privacy, Terms

---

**Key Distinction Summary:**
- **Platform** = "How it works under the hood" (for technical buyers)
- **Product** = "What the interface looks like" (for end users)
- **Solutions** = "What problem it solves for me" (for business buyers)
- **Pricing** = "What it costs"
- **Company** = "Who you are and why I should trust you"

This structure ensures technical evaluators, end users, and business buyers all find their relevant content within 2 clicks.
