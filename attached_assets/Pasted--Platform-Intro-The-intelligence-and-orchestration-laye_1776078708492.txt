## Platform

**Intro**
The intelligence and orchestration layer underneath IntegrateWise.

**Purpose**
Platform explains how IntegrateWise processes truth, context, and session summaries behind the scenes so the product can work with continuity, confidence, and governance.

**What to cover**

* Connectors and ingestion
* Truth from systems
* Context processing from conversations and activity
* Session summaries and reusable memory
* TruthLayer confidence scoring
* Evidence and freshness
* Governance and approvals
* Security and controls

**Core message**
IntegrateWise does not just connect tools. The platform turns fragmented data, human context, and approved AI outputs into a governed intelligence layer.

**Suggested page headline**
Built on truth, context, and governed memory.

**Suggested subheadline**
The IntegrateWise platform ingests records from your systems, context from your conversations, and approved session summaries — then turns them into a reliable layer for reasoning, scoring, and approvals.
