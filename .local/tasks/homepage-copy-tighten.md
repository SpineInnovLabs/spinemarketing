# Tighten Homepage Copy

## What & Why
The homepage has several wordy passages that slow readers down. Apply concise, high-signal copy principles across all sections — cut tool name lists to category phrases, collapse multi-sentence descriptions to their core idea, and strip filler from subheadlines.

## Done looks like
- Hero subheadline no longer lists 15 individual tool names; replaced with a short, punchy phrase
- Spine "Session Summaries" card description trimmed to one clear sentence
- TruthLayer subheadline paragraph cut from four sentences to one or two that land harder
- Final CTA subheadline is one clean sentence under 20 words
- Day One outcomes list — padded items tightened without losing meaning
- No section has a paragraph longer than 2 sentences

## Out of scope
- Any structural or design changes
- Pricing section copy
- Header or footer copy
- Changes to section headlines (h1/h2)

## Tasks
1. **Hero subheadline** — Replace the long tool-name list with a compact phrase (e.g. "your CRM, billing, support, docs, and chat") and trim the overall sentence to under 25 words.
2. **Spine section cards** — Shorten the "Session Summaries" card desc to one sentence; review "Truth" and "Context" cards for any redundancy and tighten if needed.
3. **TruthLayer subheadline paragraph** — Collapse the four-sentence paragraph to one or two punchy lines that convey the same governance idea without repetition.
4. **Day One outcomes** — Cut padding from the list items, especially the last item ("that's the architecture, not an add-on").
5. **Final CTA subheadline** — Rewrite to a single, direct sentence under 20 words.
